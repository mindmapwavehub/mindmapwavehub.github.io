About Mindmap WaveHub

Mindmap WaveHub is a research hub founded in 2023. We support researchers, students and organisations across different fields.

We work mainly with artificial intelligence and machine learning, data analytics, programming, and engineering software tools. We apply these to projects in engineering, computer science, health, business, the social sciences and other areas.

We help with research design, data analysis, modelling, writing, and editing and formatting. We also run webinars and hands-on workshops, and our team can present findings in person at companies in Ghana and abroad.

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different. We give you a quote based on the work involved, the level (undergraduate, master's, PhD or company project) and your deadline. You'll know the cost before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the work. Tell us your deadline when you get in touch, and we'll agree a timeline with you at the start.

How do I pay?

Payment terms are agreed directly between you and us when we confirm your plan and quote.

Is my work kept confidential?

Yes. We never share your project, data or personal details with anyone else.

Who do you work with?

Undergraduate, master's and PhD students, researchers, and companies and organisations, in Ghana and abroad. We work online, so location isn't a barrier.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. We can meet you anywhere in Ghana, and our team can come to your company for meetings or presentations. We can also travel abroad when needed. For travel outside Ghana, we agree with you beforehand how the travel costs will be covered.

Can I get help with just one part of my project?

Yes. You can choose a single service, such as data analysis or formatting, or combine several.

What do I need to get started?

Your topic or project brief, any guidelines from your institution or organisation, and your deadline. Send them by WhatsApp, email or the form.
