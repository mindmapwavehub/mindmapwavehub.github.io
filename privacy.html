Privacy note

Last updated: October 2026

This note explains what information Mindmap WaveHub collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, WhatsApp and phone numbers, whether you're enquiring for an institution, a company or yourself, the name of your institution or company, the service you need, your topic, stage and deadline, and any message you add.

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the details you send there.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to work with you on your project. We don't sell your details or share them for marketing.

Who handles it

Messages from the contact form are delivered to our email by Formspree, a form service. Formspree processes the message only to deliver it to us.

Your project and data

Any work, data or documents you share with us stay confidential. We never share them with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to respond to you and complete any work we agree on.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Email mindmapwavehub@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
